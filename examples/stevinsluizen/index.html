Stevinsluizen Salt Intrusion Simulation

Unsteady phase-wise lock exchange modeling with Deltares D-SLF (pyzsf)

1. Overview & Objectives

The Stevinsluis at Den Oever is a primary navigation lock in the Afsluitdijk connecting the saline Waddenzee to freshwater Lake IJsselmeer. During each locking cycle, water and salt are exchanged across the barrier through density currents and vessel displacement.

This example uses the Deltares D-SLF (Dutch Shipping Lock Fluxes / pyzsf) unsteady solver to evaluate annual salt intrusion fluxes across three BIVAS traffic scenarios: 2023 Reference 2050 HSK (High Climate Scenario) 2050 LVK (Low Climate Scenario).

3. Lock Geometry & Boundary Conditions

Sluiskolk Stevinsluis (FIS Data)

  • Lock Chamber ID: 8988 (Lock 43389)
  • Source: FIS-EURIS Dataset (Zenodo: 20793663)
  • Chamber Length: 120.0 m
  • Chamber Width: 13.5 m (effective area: 1,620 m²)
  • Gate Width: 13.0 m
  • Sill Depth: NAP −3.5 m (sea and lake side)
  • Chamber Floor: NAP −4.0 m (effective sill height: 0.5 m)
  • Operational Limit: Locking halts when water level > NAP +1.80 m

Boundary Conditions

  • Lake Side (IJsselmeer):
    • Water level reference: head_lake = 0.0 m
    • Salinity: salinity_lake = 1.0 kg/m³ (freshwater)
    • Temperature: 15.0 °C
  • Sea Side (Waddenzee):
    • Water level: Dynamic tidal head difference head_sea per lockage
    • Salinity: salinity_sea = 28.0 kg/m³ (saline marine)
    • Temperature: 15.0 °C

4. Phase-wise Locking Cycle (D-SLF)

Phase / Routine Physical Process Governing Parameters D-SLF API Call
Routine 1 Leveling lock chamber to lake level t_level (s), head_sea (m) z.step_phase_1(t_level, **params)
Routine 2 Doors open to Lake IJsselmeer; density exchange & ship egress/ingress t_open_lake (s), ship_volume_lake_to_sea (m³) z.step_phase_2(t_open_lake, **params)
Routine 3 Leveling lock chamber to sea level t_level (s), head_sea (m) z.step_phase_3(t_level, **params)
Routine 4 Doors open to Waddenzee; density exchange & ship egress/ingress t_open_sea (s), ship_volume_sea_to_lake (m³) z.step_phase_4(t_open_sea, **params)
